After creating the private space, private network, and connections to
external networks, you can configure the private space by:

-   ps-config-ssl-endpoints.xml

-   ps-config-domains.xml

-   ps-config-fw-rules.xml

-   ps-config-env.xml

-   ps-config-advanced.xml

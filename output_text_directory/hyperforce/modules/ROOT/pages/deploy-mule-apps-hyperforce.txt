When deploying Mule Apps to a Salesforce Hyperforce (Hyperforce)
instance, you deploy on CloudHub 2.0 to a private or shared space.
Consider the following guidelines.

Before You Begin

Before deploying Mule apps to Hyperforce, review the following points:

-   Hyperforce Instance
    Your MuleSoft account must have access to a Hyperforce instance.
    Contact your MuleSoft account representative to provision a MuleSoft
    license to a Hyperforce instance.

-   Hyperforce Endpoints
    Hyperforce has a different base URL, exchange and platform
    endpoints. For a successful deployment change the Anypoint Platform
    endpoints URLs to the supported Hyperforce regions endpoints:

    -   Configuring Mule to access Hyperforce

    -   Publish an Asset to Exchange Using Maven

    -   Configure Hyperforce in Anypoint Code Builder

    -   Configure Hyperforce in Anypoint Studio

Deploy Mule Apps to Hyperforce

To deploy a Mule app to a Hyperforce instance, deploy the app to a
CloudHub 2.0 private or shared space. For details, refer to Deploying to
CloudHub 2.0 and Shared Global Regions.

Note that deploying to CloudHub is not supported.
